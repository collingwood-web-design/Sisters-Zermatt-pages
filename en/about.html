About The Sisters

Thank you for choosing The Sisters Zermatt. We are two women from Zermatt, sisters and mothers, and together we run two charming hotels opposite each other in the heart of Zermatt. We continue our family’s passion for hospitality, standing for warmth, care, and attention to detail.

For hundreds of years, it has been the strong women of Zermatt who, with their tireless dedication, care, and passion, have shaped Zermatt’s spirit of hospitality. Just as these values were passed on to our mother, grandmother, and great-grandmother, they were instilled in us from birth, and we, in turn, pass them on to our entire Charming team.

We invite our guests to feel welcome far from home and at home with us in the mountains. We hope that your time out in Zermatt with us will be an unforgettable experience.

From our hearts

The Sisters Zermatt
