Complimentary pick-up service

Operating hours: 07:00 – 22:00

We are pleased to offer you a complimentary pick-up service from Zermatt train station to the hotel on your arrival and departure days. Our service is available daily from 07:00 to 22:00.

Please provide your exact arrival time already during the online pre-check-in. This allows us to pick you up from the station on time with our electric car and make your journey as pleasant and relaxed as possible.

If your plans change spontaneously or you are delayed, please inform us via WhatsApp or give us a call: +41 27 966 77 00.

Thank you for your cooperation!

Gädi Spa

A place to retreat for body and soul: in our Gädi Spa you’ll find soothing relaxation — whether in the comforting warmth of the sauna, the gentle steam bath, the bubbling jacuzzi, or snuggled up in Heidi’s little hay bed. Here you’re free to let go, breathe deeply, and recharge. Our spa is exclusively open to guests aged 16 and over.
