Hotel Chesa Valese

At the foot of the majestic Matterhorn, in the heart of Zermatt – rich in tradition yet wonderfully modern – we welcome you to our cosy, snug home. The clear air and the scent of local alpine flowers surround you while we serve you a wonderful glass of wine. In winter, relax by the warming fireplace with the gentle crackling of wood in your ears; in summer, unwind in our idyllic garden with the sound of mountain water murmuring in the background.

We look forward to welcoming you into our little fairytale world – in our beloved Valais and in our small yet exquisite Hotel Chesa Valese.

With us, you’ll find the perfect room for your needs and experience authentic Alpine chic combined with all the benefits of modern comfort.

Be Charming Spa

Daily open from 4pm to 9pm.

If you would like to use the spa earlier, just ask at the reception, we will open it specifically for you.

As it is a clothing-free area, we warmly welcome all guests aged 16 and over!
