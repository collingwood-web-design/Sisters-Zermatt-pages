Hotel Daniela

Relaxed and refreshingly light – that’s life at Hotel Daniela, just like being at home. In the heart of the Matterhorn village, you can simply enjoy your stay without any fuss. The fireplace in the lobby invites you to get cosy on cold winter days, and a visit to the authentic Gädi Spa is a true experience of Zermatt’s mountain culture of days gone by.

The newly renovated, family-run hotel is stylish and modern, yet still wonderfully Swiss and homely. Authentic details decorate the hotel and bring back that feeling of “Grandma’s living room” at home. Feel completely at home in the mountains!

Find your room with an incomparable breakfast buffet and spend wonderful moments in the magical Zermatt mountains.
