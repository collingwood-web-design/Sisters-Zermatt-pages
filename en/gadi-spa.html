Opening hoursDaily, 4:00 pm – 8:00 pm

We warmly welcome all guests aged 16 and over!

In the mornings from 9:00 am to 12:00 pm, children are also welcome to come and swim in the Gädi Spa.

Escape everyday life and immerse yourself in the magic of times gone by.
At the Gädi Spa, you can relax in the warm jacuzzi, sauna or steam bath – surrounded by the charm of old Zermatt.
Snuggle into Heidi’s hay beds, breathe in the scent of the Alps and let yourself drift away beneath the clear starry sky.

Here, wellness becomes a journey through time.

We are happy to book a deeply relaxing massage for you on request.
