Clause 4 below applies with regard to cancellation costs.

4.1 General conditions

Cancellations or rebookings are only valid if they are sent in writing to the booked hotel, HOTEL DANIELA or CHESA VALESE and are confirmed in writing by the respective hotel. The following deadlines apply:
• Changes/cancellations 7 days before arrival are free of charge
• Exceptions are possible by written agreement

4.1.1 Deposits Deposits already paid will be refunded in the form of vouchers for direct bookers.
4.1.2 Separate arrangements (e.g. for group bookings) may be agreed, but must be made in writing.

4.2 Cancellation date and insurance

The date on which the written declaration is received by the booking office is decisive for calculating the cancellation date.
In cases of hardship, the cancellation costs may be covered by cancellation insurance, provided that the GUEST has taken out such insurance.

4.3 Provision of a replacement person

If the booked arrangement cannot be commenced, the HOTEL DANIELA and the HOTEL CHESA VALESE will accept a replacement person who will take over the existing booking under the same conditions. If it is a direct booking and was booked directly via our website, the direct booking GUEST will be refunded the amount paid in the form of a voucher. However, if the GUEST has booked via a travel agency or an online portal outside of our website www.sisterszermatt.ch, the GUEST should only claim on their travel cancellation insurance.

In any case, we strongly recommend that the GUEST takes out travel cancellation insurance so that the GUEST is covered in such a case. The hotel accepts no liability in the event of force majeure or unreachability of the village of Zermatt.
