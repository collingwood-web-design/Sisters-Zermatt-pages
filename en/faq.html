Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about staying at Hotel Chesa Valese and Hotel Daniela in Zermatt.

Getting here & check-in

Where are Hotel Chesa Valese and Hotel Daniela located?

Both hotels are in the heart of car-free Zermatt, directly opposite each other on Steinmattstrasse: Hotel Chesa Valese at Steinmattstrasse 30 and Hotel Daniela at Steinmattstrasse 39, 3920 Zermatt, Switzerland. Zermatt train station is about 600 metres away.

How do I get from Zermatt train station to the hotel?

On your arrival and departure days we pick you up free of charge at Zermatt train station with our hotel electric car and take you back again. Please let us know your arrival time in the online pre-check-in. If you are delayed, reach us on WhatsApp or by phone at +41 27 966 77 00.

Can I drive to Zermatt?

Zermatt is car-free. If you travel by car, you park in Täsch and take the shuttle train to Zermatt. At Zermatt station we pick you up with our hotel electric car.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is possible from 14:00 on the day of arrival and check-out is until 11:00 on the day of departure, unless agreed otherwise.

Rooms & suites

Which rooms have a Matterhorn view?

At Hotel Chesa Valese, the Alpine Double Room Matterhorn View and the Alpine Matterhorn Suite have a south-facing balcony with a view of the Matterhorn. At Hotel Daniela, the Swiss Friends Suite offers Matterhorn views.

Which rooms are suitable for families and groups?

At Hotel Chesa Valese: the Alpine Roof Suite (up to 4 guests, 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms on two levels) and the Alpine Matterhorn Suite (up to 4 guests, 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, Matterhorn view). At Hotel Daniela: the Swiss Family Suite (two double rooms with a connecting door for up to 4 adults and 1–2 children) and the Swiss Friends Suite (2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms). For three guests there are the Alpine Triple Room and the Swiss Double Room Plus.

What is included in the room rate?

Your rate includes the pick-up and drop-off service from Zermatt station by hotel electric car, The Sisters breakfast buffet (7:30–10:30), access to the hotel spa, bathrobe and slippers on request, a minibar with soft drinks, a Nespresso coffee machine, TV and Wi-Fi.

Is Wi-Fi free?

Yes, Wi-Fi is free for our guests in both hotels.

Breakfast & spa

Is breakfast included?

Yes. The Sisters breakfast buffet is included for all hotel guests and served daily from 7:30 to 10:30, with regional, high-quality products.

What does the Be Charming Spa at Hotel Chesa Valese offer?

The Be Charming Spa has a sauna (80–90 °C) and a steam bath, and we are happy to arrange a massage on request. It is open daily from 16:00 to 20:00; if you would like to use it earlier, just ask at reception. The spa is clothing-free and for guests aged 16 and over.

What does the Gädi Spa at Hotel Daniela offer?

The Gädi Spa offers a sauna, a gentle steam bath, a bubbling jacuzzi and Heidi’s cosy hay beds. It is open daily from 16:00 to 20:00 for guests aged 16 and over.

Booking & cancellation

How can I book a room?

The easiest way is to book directly on our website with the “Book a room” button. You can also call us on +41 27 966 8080 or email sisters@sisterszermatt.ch.

What is the cancellation policy?

Changes and cancellations are free of charge up to 7 days before arrival. They must be sent in writing to the booked hotel. Non-refundable rates cannot be refunded. Full details are in our terms and conditions.

Do you offer gift vouchers?

Yes – you can order The Sisters value voucher online, a lovely gift for a stay in Zermatt.

About The Sisters

What is the difference between Hotel Chesa Valese and Hotel Daniela?

Hotel Chesa Valese combines Alpine chic with a fireplace, an idyllic garden and the Be Charming Spa with sauna and steam bath. Hotel Daniela is a renovated family hotel with authentic Swiss character – traditional paper-cut art, rustic wood – and the Gädi Spa with jacuzzi and hay beds. Both are 4-star hotels, stand opposite each other and serve The Sisters breakfast.

Who runs The Sisters hotels?

Rebecca and Sophia Julen – two women from Zermatt, sisters and mothers. They continue the tradition of the strong women who have shaped Zermatt’s hospitality for generations.
